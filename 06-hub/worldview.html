世界观速览

Worldview Overview

下面是整套宇宙设定的精华速览，完整内容见正典库（卷1《概念协议》· 卷2《世界观圣经》）。

A condensed overview of the whole setting; the full canon lives in Vol.1 (Concept Protocol) and Vol.2 (Worldview Bible).

一句话

In One Line

宇宙默认熵增，靠周期性的 序态自稳系统（原「负熵稳定系统」）纠错维持低熵；取经就是一次纠错心跳，八十一难就是系统的一次次单元测试。

The universe defaults to entropy increase and stays low-entropy through periodic error correction of the Order-State Self-Stabilization System (formerly the "negentropy stabilization system"); the pilgrimage is one error-correction heartbeat, and the eighty-one trials are the system's unit tests.

权限受限原理

Permission-Restriction Principle

Root / Admin 不能直接修改高维规则，只能借低维进程触发；紧箍咒 = 维度锁。这是「强者不能平白无敌」的物理根基——再强的能力也必须落在合法权限或受限授权内。

Root / Admin cannot directly rewrite high-dimensional rules; they may only trigger through low-dimensional processes; the headband = a dimension lock. This is the physical basis of "the strong cannot be invincible for free" — even overwhelming power must land within legitimate permission or restricted authorization.

观象域（投影幕布层）

Observational Mantle (projection layer)

人类所见《西游记》的神话镜像（腾云驾雾、七十二变等）发生在观象域，是宇宙本尊经神话滤镜降维投影的影像，不是宇宙本身。

The mythic mirror of Journey to the West seen by humans (cloud-riding, seventy-two transformations, etc.) occurs in the Observational Mantle — a projection of the universe itself, filtered down through a mythic filter; it is not the universe itself.

术语红线Terminology red line：五层膜 = 真实结构本尊；观象域 = 神话投影镜像。二者不混写。Five layers = the real structure; Observational Mantle = mythic projection image. Never conflate them.

All three modes are different presentation filters over the same reality — change the filter, not the physics. Pick a home mode when you create.
