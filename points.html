维度积分（贡献力计算）

Dimension Points (contribution calculus)

维度Dimension内容Content权重Weight
C1 · 创作量Volume入正典内容量 / 有效提案数canonical content / valid proposals30%
C2 · 质量分Quality社区评议加权（评审团 + 观察者投票）community-weighted review (jury + observer votes)30%

各维度归一化后加权求和 → 维度积分。入正典内容标记贡献者身份与当次积分，累入其账户。防刷：质量分由社区加权而非单一点赞，异常聚类触发复核。

Each dimension is normalized, then weighted-summed → dimension points. Canonized content records the contributor and the points awarded, accumulated into their account. Anti-abuse: quality is community-weighted, not a single like; anomalous clusters trigger re-review.

收益分配（原则）

Revenue Distribution (Principles)

商业化衍生收益进入总池，未来按贡献分配。核心原则：创意与生态并重、奖励长期投入、分配透明可审计、总量守恒。具体比例与方向待商业化实际启动后依社区共识确定。

Revenue enters a total pool and will be distributed by contribution. Core principles: creativity and ecosystem on equal footing; rewarding long-term investment; transparent and auditable; conservation of total. Specific ratios and directions will be set upon commercialization by community consensus.

积分怎么用

What Points Do

  • 收益分配基数（未来按贡献分配，具体细则待商业化启动再定）
  • 共创角色晋升：观察者 → 正式共创者 → 评审团
  • 模态坍缩投票加权：影响 W(A/B/C) 权重周期重算
  • Revenue allocation base (by contribution; details set upon commercialization)
  • Role advancement: observer → co-creator → jury
  • Mode-collapse vote weighting: affects W(A/B/C) periodic recompute
模态坍缩底线Mode-collapse floor： 三模态权重最低不低于 15%（多样性底线），单一模态不会被完全边缘化；你的创作持续计入，可触发该模态的坍缩收敛。 no mode weight drops below 15% (diversity floor); no single mode is fully marginalized; your ongoing creation counts and can trigger that mode's collapse-convergence.
